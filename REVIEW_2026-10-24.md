Source: Erdős Problem a Day (the maintainer of <https://erdosproblemaday.com/>), replying to
the #86 submission of this repository.  Verbatim key excerpts:

> **Verdict: VERIFIED — as a certificate: both graphs have 1,532 edges and no 4-cycle, so
> ex(Q9, C4) ≥ 1,532. One correction to the context, which you anticipated: the number 1,532

> Every row is a Q9 edge, none repeated; none of the C(9,2)·2^7 = 4,608 square faces has all
> four edges; no two vertices share two neighbours. Adding the missing edge of a three-edge
> face is rejected by both tests. (In a hypercube every 4-cycle is a square face.)

> A second instrument, different parser and method: adjacency matrix A, off-diagonal entries
> of A² at most 1. Every face carries one or three edges (784 and 3,824 faces) — the
> odd-square, fully frustrated signature. The two graphs share 1,024 edges.
>
> Under Marinari–Parisi–Ritort's coupling J(x, x+e_ν) = (−1)^(Σ_{μ<ν} x_μ), has exactly your
> 1,532 edges as its satisfied bonds, with energy per spin −95/192 = −0.4947916…
>
> Priority. Marinari, Parisi and Ritort, *The fully frustrated hypercubic model is glassy and
> aging at large D* (cond-mat/9410089, October 1994), p. 4: in D = 9 "we have reached a value
> very close to that, −.494792 … On this scale we have reached a value of −380". With their
> normalisation that is exactly 1,532 satisfied bonds of 2,304, and the satisfied bonds of a
> fully frustrated configuration meet every square in one or three edges — so a 1,532-edge
> C4-free subgraph of Q9 was reported to exist in 1994, with no configuration published.
> Minamoto's arXiv:2603.29127 v5 (§6) transcribes that value. Your spin state reproduces their

## What this implies for this repository

1. The certificate is independently confirmed (their own code, two instruments).
2. **Priority correction accepted**: the value 1,532 is from 1994 (MPR, cond-mat/9410089).
   The contribution of this repository is an explicit public certificate (plus a Lean check)
   at that value — not a new lower bound, not a new number.
3. The spin configuration in `spins_q9.txt` is a witness for the MPR ground-state energy at
   D = 9 (−95/192 per spin).
4. The odd-square family cap at n = 9 is 1,536; whether it is attained is open.  This
   repository does not claim it.
5. The Lean file was not part of their check; its trust caveat (`native_decide`) is disclosed
   in the README and `lean/AXIOMS.log`.
